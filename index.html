LockTrace — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

LockTrace is an anti-theft tool that helps you, the owner of a device, see who used your phone while you were away. This policy is short because the app is built so that your data never leaves your device.

The short version

  • LockTrace does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.
  • The app has no internet permission and makes no network connections.
  • Everything it records stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

What the app records, and where it stays

To do its job, LockTrace stores the following only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Photos taken by the front camera when someone enters the wrong PIN, or unlocks shortly after a failed attempt. These are encrypted with a key held in the device's hardware keystore, which never leaves the device.
  • Lock, unlock and failed-attempt events, with their times.
  • The names of apps opened during a monitored session (via Usage Access).
  • Your settings, such as which apps you mark as sensitive.

None of this is sent anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising code in the app.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera — to photograph whoever fails to unlock your phone.
  • Device admin (watch-login only) — solely to be told when a wrong PIN is entered. It cannot lock, wipe, or control your device.
  • Usage Access — to list which apps were opened during a session.
  • Display over other apps — so the camera screen can open over the lock screen.
  • Notifications — to run the monitoring service and to alert you.
  • Run at startup / ignore battery optimisation — so monitoring keeps working.

Your control over your data

  • You can delete all evidence at any time from within the app, and set how long photos and history are kept.
  • Uninstalling LockTrace permanently deletes everything it has recorded.

Intended use

LockTrace is intended for monitoring a device that you own. It always displays its app icon and an ongoing notification while running, and offers no way to hide itself.
